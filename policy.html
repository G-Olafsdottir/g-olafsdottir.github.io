Policy engagement

Alongside research I work with election observation and policy organisations, as an observer on the ground, as an adviser and consultant, and through policy writing and talks.

Roles and assignments

  • 2026–Advisory board member, Polaris Electoral Observatory
  • 2019–Election observer and statistician, election observation roster, Folke Bernadotte Academy
  • 2025Research consultant, International IDEA. Wrote the chapter on trends in electoral violence in 2024 for the Review of the 2024 Super-Cycle Year of Elections
  • 2023Short-term election observer, OSCE, seconded by the Folke Bernadotte Academy to Turkey (two election observation missions)
  • 2022–2023Research consultant, Dag Hammarskjöld Foundation. Built a database on funding streams to youth-led peacebuilding and on project impacts and outcomes
  • 2018–2022Election worker in Swedish elections, Stockholm; district co-chair in the 2022 general election
  • 2019Short-term election observer, OSCE, seconded by the Folke Bernadotte Academy to Uzbekistan
  • 2014–2015Intern and research assistant, Democracy and Development team, International IDEA
