Research

My research examines partisan dynamics in electoral authoritarian regimes, as well as how authoritarianism influences international cooperation. Specifically, my expertise focuses on electoral violence, election boycotts, and election monitoring. I combine statistical analysis with qualitative field research, including fieldwork in Turkey and Uganda.

Current projects

  • Opting out? Explaining the effectiveness of election boycotts Swedish Research Council, 2025–. With Sebastian van Baalen (project leader), Uppsala University, and Jesper Bjarnesen, the Nordic Africa Institute. We are building a global database of election boycotts, 1945–2024, to explain why boycotts occur and whether they are effective.
  • DEMCOOP: Democracy, autocracy and international cooperation Stockholm Center on Global Governance, Stockholm University. I study states’ contributions to election observation missions run by international organisations, and whether autocracies and democracies differ in how they support international election monitoring. I'm also collaborating with colleagues on a survey experiment investigating how having autocracies in human rights bodies shapes the perceived legitimacy of both the IO and the country itself.
